# Techno-Commercial Cost-Delta Analysis (1.9 K Nb-Ti vs. 50 K REBCO Architecture)

**Author:** Abhishek Singh | **Copyright (c) 2026 Abhishek Singh**

## Summary of Capital & Operational Shifts (27 km Collider Scale)

Transitioning from legacy 1.9 K superfluid helium Nb-Ti magnets to a 50 K helium-free Doped REBCO + sp<sup>2</sup>-carbon/diamond liner architecture shifts upfront CAPEX from cryogenic refrigeration plants to high-temperature superconducting conductor tape, while delivering an approximate **40x Carnot thermodynamic efficiency improvement** and **70% reduction in annual cryogenic grid OPEX**.

See `data/capex_opex_cost_delta.csv` for itemized line-by-line comparisons.
